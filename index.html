Camp Evaluation

Kellan Duncan made a very strong impression at his first Rubio Long Snapping Camp in Seattle, WA on September 20, 2026, showing outstanding mechanics and tremendous long-term potential. He has great starting form, a smooth snapping motion, and does an excellent job getting his upper body all the way through to the punter.

Kellan maintains solid balance throughout the snapping motion, and his spiral is very close to perfect, making the ball extremely easy to catch. I would love to see him continue working slow-motion drills to get his entire body fully involved because that will create much more overall speed on the ball. As he continues to add size and strength over the next couple of years, his ceiling will continue to rise.

Kellan is extremely coachable, has lots of upside, and put together a really impressive first camp. Excited to see him at VEGAS 49 on January 16-17, 2027! Really close to 3 stars!

— Rubio Long Snapping
